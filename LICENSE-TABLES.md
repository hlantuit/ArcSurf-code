# Licence of the tables and documents

The scripts in `scripts/` are licensed under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`).

The tables and documents of this repository are licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
licence (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode):

- `xwalk/`: crosswalk tables from the native units of every source to the ArcSurf attributes
- `legends/`: legend transcriptions of the Russian sheets and of the scanned Canadian maps
- `georef/`: georeferencing records of the Russian sheets
- `sources/`: source registries
- `addendum/`: documentation of the ArcSurf addendum
- `LEGEND.md`: the harmonised legend

Attribution: Lantuit, H. and Fritz, M.: ArcSurf code (see `CITATION.cff` for the version and its DOI).

The legend tables reproduce unit indices, swatch colours and unit descriptions of the source maps; the source maps
themselves remain under the terms of their publishers (Appendix A of the data paper and `sources.csv` of the data
release). The original Greenland datasets of the Geological Survey of Denmark and Greenland (GEUS) remain available
from GEUS Dataverse under CC BY-SA 4.0; the GEUS-derived component of ArcSurf is published under CC BY 4.0 with
separate permission from GEUS.
